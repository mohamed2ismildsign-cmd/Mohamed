My studying process

From brief to product

01

Research

I use the Double Diamond method. This stage takes two to four weeks.

Where I focus most
02

Sketch & ideate

The stage I give the most attention, because it shapes every stage after it.

03

Develop

I refine the strongest idea: its form, its ergonomics and how it will be made.

04

CAD & render

A full 3D model, renders and engineering drawings.

05

Prototype

A physical model or a real build, often with CNC, metal or wood.
